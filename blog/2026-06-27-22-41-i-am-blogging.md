# I Am Blogging And So Should You

I blog for spite posting. more commonly known as defensive publication. I have good ideas, and sometime great ideas, but I will never make a million dollar out of it. So why should a multi-billion dollar company make a fortune for the same idea that they would remove access to.

I blog to feel better about myself. My work doesnt want my idea so why keep it stuck in my head. why not share with with the world where like minded people can find us and we can find them.

And finally I blog to understand my ideas. Richard Feynman said that we understand our ideas when we can simply explain it. Blog allows us to share idea to the world forcing us to simplify it to its core idea, and when we can do that we have understood it.
