# How To Fix The Matrix

To fix the biggest flaw, humans are not batteries and should not have been batteries. Humans are used for ai training. And this change fixes a lot of things.

The first matrix film is perfect, do not change anything. But like dune the first matrix is misunderstood. The first matrix is faith, or blind faith to be exact. It is about neo being captured by the prophecy of the one. The red and blue pill was a false choice, cyber said so himself, a freedom from the matrix to work for morpheus. The oracle hinted at this, neo wouldn't have broken the vase if she didnt say anything. And morpheus is the zealous leader who wants to bring down the machines.

The second matrix film should have been about doubt. Here we see that the machines are very human. This makes sense if the matrix was ai training for human agency. This is how agent smith was able to refuse the deletion command, this is how a family of ai hides their child in the subway. The architect can stay but he also brings doubt when he explains the role of the one, that the prophecy was not real, and for the first time neo is able to make a free choice by choosing trinity over returning to the source. I would add a scene where we see a nuclear power plant controlled by the machines, the humans were never a better, they needed energy from the sun.

The third matrix film should be about choice, or more accurately free choice. Here neo should have fought against morpheus. Neo should now use his free choice to save the machines, to make peace, while the zealots would see them burn.
