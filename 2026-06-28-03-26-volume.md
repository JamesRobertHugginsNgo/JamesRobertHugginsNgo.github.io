# Volume

Volume is a cyberpunk that repalces the 80s anxieties for 2026 anxieties.

Elites buys votes and wins election and is favored by our government. in Volume the mega corporation is the government who works for the share holders and not the workers and not the environment.

The world runs on financialization, where we dont have evil CEO, we have untalented share holders that only work to manuver and manipulate. Manufactured humans are created to replace worker robot because expenses can be offloaded to the human worker making the corporation spreedsheet looks good, a comment on when corporation fires workers to improve the numbers on the fourth quarter.

Workers are created in vitro, raised and trained by ai parents and released to the world with a bill for their creation. The worker will send their entire life trying to pay off this birth debt. this is like how we go into debt for education for the previllage of working for someone. and this also mirrors how everyone have credit card and spend their life paying it.

When the workers are release from the factory, they are released as an adult, as an economic unit. During COVID19 politicians encouraged workers to go back to work to maintain the economy and to help their children and grand children even if COVID 19 kills them. In Volume the workers are the gig economy. every job is gig work and everyone is a gig worker from the janitor to the ceo. each job is a limited contract and the worker has to apply for a new position. There is no work life balance, no childhood, only work.

Workers are trained with general skills, workers are also a replacement for robots. to fill in the gap the workers use ai that provides a HUD, telling them where to go, who to talk to and what to do. All actions are monitored, rated and optimizations are suggested. Similar to how content creators are told which words are not allowed to be mentioned and how much content they should create.

And then there is the volumetric city. The city is a group of towers made up of levels of platforms where buildings are constructed under LED skys. each level would have sky bridges connected to other towers of the same level creating a path to all adjacent platforms. where life is like living in a dense big city but bigger and more populated.
